A $500K portfolio is the classic Lean FIRE target. It's small enough that good savers can get there by their 30s or 40s, and as of our latest data refresh, it goes much further than most people think: **52 of the 121 cities we track now have a Lean FIRE Number at or below $500K**. Good news for many folks in this financial position who are looking for the exit. The question now becomes which of these 52 qualifying places you actually want to live in.

## The 4% Rule on $500K

The 4% safe withdrawal rate means a $500K portfolio supports about **$20,000 per year**, or **$1,667 per month**. That is a very tight US cost-of-living budget. Our analysis below shows how you can live off this budget while covering an apartment, private health insurance, meals, and local transport, often with room to spare. You just have to know where to look.

| Portfolio Size | Annual Withdrawal (4%) | Monthly Budget |

| $750,000 | $30,000 | $2,500 |
| $1,000,000 | $40,000 | $3,333 |

## Where Can You Retire With $500K in 2026?

More places than you might think: 15 in Europe, 12 in Latin America, 8 in Southeast Asia, 7 in South Asia, 5 in Africa, 2 in East Asia, and one each in the Caribbean, the Middle East, and North America. Portugal, Spain, and much of Central Europe now sit comfortably under the line at the Lean FIRE tier, which puts EU healthcare systems and real retirement visas within reach of a $500K portfolio. You can see the complete picture, including what the historical backtest says about survival odds, on the [$500K breakdown page](/retire-on/500k).

Since 52 cities is too many to profile in one blog post, we selected the top ten - aka the ones with the best FIRE Score - from our data. What is Fire Score? It's a 1-10 quality-of-life composite of safety, healthcare, infrastructure, and expat friendliness. It answers a simple question, "how good will my life be if I FIRE there?" with a simple number. The full table of every qualifying city follows.

## Best Places to Retire With 500,000 Dollars

### 1. [Taipei, Taiwan](/cities/taipei)

**Monthly cost: $1,395** · **Lean FIRE Number: $418,000** · **FIRE Score: 8.3**

The highest-rated city on this list is one of Asia's best-kept FIRE secrets. Lean housing puts you in a modest studio in an MRT-served district like Zhonghe or Wenshan for about $500 a month. A robust metro system makes the whole city reachable without a car.

- **Visa:** 90 days visa-free for US citizens; a resident visa is available with proof of financial means, roughly $25K+ in savings. *Rules change - verify before applying.*
- **Healthcare:** Excellent. As a non-resident retiree you carry a private regional plan rather than National Health Insurance, and Taipei's hospitals are among the best in Asia.

### 2. [Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia](/cities/kuala-lumpur)

**Monthly cost: $1,230** · **Lean FIRE Number: $369,000** · **FIRE Score: 7.9**

The Goldilocks of Southeast Asia is more developed than Vietnam and far cheaper than Singapore. Your portfolio gets you a furnished one-bedroom condo with a pool and gym in a value area like Old Klang Road running about $460 a month.

- **Visa:** 90 days visa-free for most nationalities; the Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) program covers long-term residency, with tiers starting at a US$150K fixed deposit. *Rules change - verify before applying.*
- **Healthcare:** Excellent. A local plan buys access to Gleneagles, Prince Court, and Pantai which are the hospitals making KL a medical-tourism hub.

### 3. [Málaga, Spain](/cities/malaga)

**Monthly cost: $1,073** · **Lean FIRE Number: $322,000** · **FIRE Score: 7.8**

Here you'll get 300 days of sunshine on the Costa del Sol, but a fair warning on what Lean buys you... a room in a shared flat near the center. Even a private one-bedroom pushes you toward the FIRE tier, which the [city page](/cities/malaga) breaks down.

- **Visa:** Spain's Non-Lucrative Visa is the retirement route, one year and renewable, no work allowed. *Rules change - verify before applying.*
- **Healthcare:** Excellent. The visa requires a full-coverage, no-copay private policy from a Spanish insurer such as Adeslas or ASISA, which is exactly what the budget line buys.

### 4. [Valencia, Spain](/cities/valencia)

**Monthly cost: $1,068** · **Lean FIRE Number: $320,000** · **FIRE Score: 7.8**

Welcome to coastal Spain at half the price of Barcelona. You'll have beach paella, the City of Arts and Sciences, and one of Europe's largest urban parks. It's the same Lean housing caveat as Málaga - a shared flat at this tier.

- **Visa:** Non-Lucrative Visa with proof of sufficient funds; permanent residency and citizenship are possible after 10 years. *Rules change - verify before applying.*
- **Healthcare:** Excellent, on the same no-copay Adeslas or ASISA private policies the visa requires.

### 5. [Quebec City, Canada](/cities/quebec-city)

**Monthly cost: $1,525** · **Lean FIRE Number: $458,000** · **FIRE Score: 7.7**

Here you'll find the closest thing to Europe in North America. It's a UNESCO-listed Old Town and four real seasons. Your portfolio gets you a studio or old one-bedroom in Limoilou or Saint-Sauveur, running you about $725.

- **Visa:** No visa needed for Canadians, but there isn't a clean retirement pathway for Americans. This place is best for Canadian readers or dual citizens. *Rules change - verify before applying.*
- **Healthcare:** Excellent. RAMQ covers essentials for eligible Quebec residents, topped with a small extended-health plan and the mandatory drug-plan premium.

### 6. [Prague, Czech Republic](/cities/prague)

**Monthly cost: $1,396** · **Lean FIRE Number: $419,000** · **FIRE Score: 7.7**

Experience medieval architecture, famous beer culture, and costs well below Western Europe. You'll live in a modest studio in a well-connected district like Žižkov or Vršovice, costing you roughly $681.

- **Visa:** 90 days Schengen visa-free, then a long-term visa for financially independent persons with proof of funds; permanent residency is possible after 5 years. *Rules change - verify before applying.*
- **Healthcare:** Good. Czech residence permits require comprehensive commercial coverage; entry-level policies from insurers like PVZP satisfy it.

### 7. [Barcelona, Spain](/cities/barcelona)

**Monthly cost: $1,206** · **Lean FIRE Number: $362,000** · **FIRE Score: 7.7**

You have the beach, Gaudí architecture, and gastronomy all in one city. Barcelona is pricier than Málaga or Valencia, and Lean housing is a room in a shared flat in Sants or outer Gràcia. The fact that Barcelona can qualify at all under $500K is the headline here.

- **Visa:** Non-Lucrative Visa, same as the rest of Spain. Sufficient funds, no work. *Rules change - verify before applying.*
- **Healthcare:** Excellent, through the same private-policy route as Málaga and Valencia.

### 8. [Tallinn, Estonia](/cities/tallinn)

**Monthly cost: $1,358** · **Lean FIRE Number: $407,000** · **FIRE Score: 7.5**

You'll be in a UNESCO medieval Old Town run by Europe's most digital government. Lodging is a renovated one-bedroom in Kristiine or Mustamäe which runs about $638.

- **Visa:** 90 days Schengen visa-free; a D-visa covers longer stays with proof of financial means. *Rules change - verify before applying.*
- **Healthcare:** Good. An entry private plan with the 30,000+ euro coverage Schengen residence rules require.

### 9. [Lisbon, Portugal](/cities/lisbon)

**Monthly cost: $1,121** · **Lean FIRE Number: $336,000** · **FIRE Score: 7.5**

Here you find Europe's FIRE capital, largely because of the visa advantage. The D7 is built for passive-income retirees and leads to citizenship in 5 years. Lean housing resembles a room in a shared flat in Arroios or Benfica. Want a private one-bedroom? Look a tier up at full-on FIRE.

- **Visa:** D7 Passive Income Visa, renewable, with a path to citizenship in 5 years. *Rules change - verify before applying.*
- **Healthcare:** Good. An entry policy from a Portuguese insurer such as Multicare or Medis satisfies the D7 requirement.

### 10. [Porto, Portugal](/cities/porto)

**Monthly cost: $1,011** · **Lean FIRE Number: $303,000** · **FIRE Score: 7.5**

Take everything Lisbon offers at the lowest cost of any city in this top ten. With $303,000 needed, the same D7 visa advantage, and the same healthcare system as above, you can find yourself in a riverside old town where your budget stretches noticeably further.

- **Visa:** D7 Passive Income Visa, identical to Lisbon. *Rules change - verify before applying.*
- **Healthcare:** Good, through the same Multicare or Medis entry policies.

## How to Retire Early on $500K: What Actually Matters

We've done the math, but that's the easy part. Choosing among 52 places matters much more at this budget than it does at higher tiers:

- **Visa reality, not visa marketing.** The best cities on this list pair affordability with a real retirement visa: Portugal's D7, Spain's Non-Lucrative, Malaysia's MM2H. Others rely on tourist-visa cycling, which works today and can change overnight. Verify the current rules before committing.
- **What Lean housing actually is.** At the $1,667 monthly envelope, the European entries price a room in a shared flat or a modest studio, not a two-bedroom with a view. If that is not your retirement, your real number is the FIRE tier, roughly double; the [$1M guide](/blog/best-cities-to-retire-on-1-million) covers it.
- **Healthcare floor.** You are buying entry-level private cover, which handles routine and most specialist care well in every featured city. Complex cases in the cheapest cities often mean traveling to a regional hub.
- **Buffer beats precision.** Retiring on exactly your city's number leaves nothing for market shocks. Picking a city $100K below your portfolio, which 52 choices make easy, is the simplest safety margin there is.
